说We're also building something

Tonelet — an AI Mandarin pronunciation coach.

Most Mandarin learning apps teach you words. Almost none of them teach you to be understood. Tonelet is the AI we're building to fix that — it listens to how you speak Mandarin and tells you, syllable by syllable, what's actually wrong with your tones and pronunciation.

To train it well, we need real voices. We're recruiting British learners of Mandarin to record short clips, and native Chinese speakers to grade them. The recordings teach the AI what British people actually sound like — and what good Mandarin sounds like.

We're looking for two roles:

  • British Mandarin learners — any level, from absolute beginner to advanced. You'll record short Mandarin clips on a simple web app.
  • Native Chinese speakers as judges — fluent native speakers who can grade recordings on pronunciation, tones, and fluency.

What you'll get: Free early access to Tonelet when it launches, in return for completing the recordings. Judges are paid for their time.
