Last updated: 21 May 2026

In plain English

You've signed up to receive a free Mandarin guide and ongoing newsletters from us. We'll email you Mandarin learning content and updates about Tonelet, the AI Mandarin pronunciation app we're building. We don't sell your email or share it with advertisers. You can unsubscribe any time using the link in any email.

1. Who we are

This newsletter is operated by Tonelet Ltd, trading as Chinese with Harry. We are a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17228833) with registered office at 71-75 Shelton Street, Covent Garden, London, WC2H 9JQ.

Tonelet Ltd is the data controller for the personal data described in this notice. We are registered with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (registration: pending).

For any questions about your data, contact us at chinesewithharry@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Your email address
  • Your first name (if you choose to provide it)
  • Engagement data — which emails you open and which links you click, collected automatically by Beehiiv, our newsletter platform
  • Approximate country-level location, inferred from your IP address by Beehiiv for analytics

3. Why we collect it

  • To send you the free Mandarin guide you signed up for
  • To send you the ongoing Chinese with Harry newsletter
  • To contact you about Tonelet, our AI Mandarin pronunciation app, including when it launches — signing up means you've expressed interest in the app
  • To understand which newsletter content is most useful, so we can improve it

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose outside the newsletter and Tonelet itself.

4. Our lawful basis

We rely on your consent under UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a). You give consent when you submit the signup form. You can withdraw it at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email — no penalty, no questions asked.

5. Who we share your data with

We share your personal data only with:

  • Beehiiv — our newsletter platform. They host the newsletter, store the subscriber list, and send emails on our behalf. Beehiiv processes data in the United States under appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers, marketers, or any other third party.

6. International transfers

Beehiiv is based in the United States. Your data is therefore transferred outside the UK. These transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards including the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses, as applicable.

7. How long we keep your data

  • While you're subscribed — your email and engagement data are held by us and by Beehiiv
  • If you unsubscribe — your email is removed from our active list. Beehiiv may retain anonymised analytics
  • If you request deletion under your GDPR rights, we'll remove your data within 30 days

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data — get a copy of what we hold
  • Rectification — correct any inaccurate data
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data
  • Restrict processing — pause our use of your data while a query is resolved
  • Object to our processing
  • Data portability — get your data in a machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, with no penalty

The quickest way to withdraw consent is to click unsubscribe in any email. For other requests, email chinesewithharry@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days.
